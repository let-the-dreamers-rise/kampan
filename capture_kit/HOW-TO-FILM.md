# Kampan: what to film (about 25 minutes)

These clips are the real-world evidence for the OpenCV entry. Film them whenever you can. Upload them to
`D:\kampan\clips\` on the laptop (any file names, the list below says which is which).

## Setup

1. Print `kampan-markers-A4.pdf` at 100% scale (turn off "fit to page"). Check the 100 mm bar with a ruler.
   No printer: tell me, and we use a ruler in the frame instead.
2. Cut out marker id 0 and marker id 1 (each 40 mm square, keep the white border).
3. Phone: slow-motion mode, 240 fps if it has it, otherwise 120 fps. Turn off "auto slow-mo" trimming if your phone has it.
4. Rest the phone on a stack of books or a tripod. Do not hold it in your hand for the main clips.
5. Good light. Daylight or a bright lamp. Slow-motion gets dark fast.
6. Every clip: 10 seconds, the machine filling about half the frame, no people in the frame.

## Clips

| # | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fan, healthy, speed 3 | Table or pedestal fan. Tape marker 0 flat on the motor housing (the part behind the blades that does not spin). Tape marker 1 on the wall or a box behind the fan, facing the camera. Camera 40-60 cm away, facing the housing straight on. |
| 2 | Fan, unbalanced, speed 3 | Same setup. Switch the fan off, tape a coin (or two) near the tip of ONE blade, switch on, film. |
| 3 | Fan, healthy, speed 1 | Remove the coin. Speed 1. |
| 4 | Fan, unbalanced, speed 1 | Coin back on, speed 1. |
| 5 | Fan, unbalanced, speed 3, far | Same as clip 2, but camera about 2 metres away. This one is meant to be weak: the agent should ask you to come closer. |
| 6 | Fan, unbalanced, speed 3, handheld | Same as clip 2, but hold the phone in your hand. The agent should catch the shake. |
| 7 | Speaker tone 47 Hz | Play `tone-47hz.wav` on a Bluetooth speaker at high volume (laptop or phone plays, speaker sits on the table). Tape marker 0 on the speaker body or its rubber passive radiator. Marker 1 on something still behind it. Film 10 s. |
| 8 | Speaker tone 31 Hz | Same with `tone-31hz.wav`. |
| 9 | Speaker tone 73 Hz | Same with `tone-73hz.wav`. |
| 10 | Optional: washing machine spin | Marker 0 on the front panel, marker 1 on the wall. Note the spin speed setting (rpm). |
| 11 | You, for the video | Normal (not slow-mo) selfie video, 10-15 s: "I'm Ashwin Goyal, a computer science student at VIT Bhopal, and this is Kampan." |

## Write down

- Your phone model and the slow-motion setting you used (240 or 120 fps).
- For each fan clip: the fan's speed setting and how many blades it has.
- For clip 10: the spin speed setting.

No speaker? Skip 7-9. No fan? Any machine with a motor works: a mixer-grinder at low speed on a table,
a cooler, a pump. Safety first: keep fingers and the phone away from moving parts, and do not open any guard.
